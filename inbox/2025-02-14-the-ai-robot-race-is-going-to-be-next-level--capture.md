On Wednesday, Apple analyst Ming-Chi Kuo shared in an X post that Apple is exploring both humanoid and non-humanoid robots. As its Vision Pro failed to gain much interest, and it failed to make an EV smart car with AV capabilities (like Xiaomi has achieved in China), it needs to dream big. Humanoid robots is a race that Apple needs to be a major player in. Whatever happens in Emergent AI and LLMs, robots are going to be an increasing part of the human story, of our cities, of our commerce and manufacturing and increasingly a consumer market for social robots. It’s my opinion that it won’t be the likes of Tesla or OpenAI who lead robotics, but rather companies like Google, Nvidia, Apple and Amazon who will end up acquiring the best robotics startups.
