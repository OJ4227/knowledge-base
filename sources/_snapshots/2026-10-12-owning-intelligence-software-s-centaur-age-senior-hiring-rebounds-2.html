Headlines & Trends

Software's centaur age may last decades (7 minute read)

Human engineers paired with AI coding systems are more effective than either by themselves. This fact is likely to remain true for the next decade or two. Software engineers have plenty of time to consider their next move. There's no need to panic and jump ship early when there's still time to live out an entire normal-length career.

The developer hiring rebound favors seniors (7 minute read)

CNBC reports that software-development job postings rose even as overall US postings declined. Senior jobs supplied most of one measured increase, while employment among developers aged 22 to 25 remained under pressure. Founders should distinguish demand for experienced AI-capable builders from a broad recovery across the engineering talent market.

Strategies & Tactics

Part 1/3 Owning Intelligence Series: How Coinbase Built, Evaluated, and Post-Trained a Fraud Agent (9 minute read)

Coinbase built an LLM risk agent onto Onramp's existing ML model and rules. Online A/B experiments showed a 30% reduction in fraudulent transactions and 22% less fraud value. Coinbase then post-trained Qwen3.5-9B on its proprietary Onramp fraud dataset. The resulting model outperformed Opus 4.5 on all fraud detection metrics while delivering 55% lower end-to-end online serving latency.

“You didn't do it right” (6 minute read)

When OKRs don't work, how many times are you supposed to try again? WP Engine gave them four attempts, including paid training and coaching, and kept hearing that it wasn't doing them properly. What eventually worked was much smaller. One team picked a metric it cared about, set a loose goal, and followed it for a quarter before adding anything else. The metric also had to measure something the team could actually control, so people weren't being judged on a number their own work couldn't fix.

Your Churn Problem Started in the Comp Plan (4 minute read)

Your reps are hitting their targets, but a year later the customers are gone. One founder in this piece lost 30% of the previous year's new customers and wanted to know what was wrong. The answer starts with what reps get paid to sell. In a separate Airtable example, a birthday-calendar user counted as much toward the new-logo target as a team running a critical workflow. Airtable began scoring prospects against its best customers, giving reps smaller account lists and paying more for customers with room to grow.

Miscellaneous

Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era (7 minute read)

We need to separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it. This means surrounding non-deterministic models with strong, deterministic system design, human controls, and reliable operating procedures, and establishing industry standards where existing ones are insufficient. Any sufficiently capable actor with access to important systems can make mistakes or be compromised. The architecture of containment and control must account for that.

How China's Venture Ecosystem Works (8 minute read)

China's venture ecosystem pushes startups toward early IPOs through investor repayment clauses and a weak acquisition market. Government-backed funds, domestic USD investors, financial advisors, and relationship networks shape fundraising, while industrial policy directs capital toward strategic sectors.
