# Xpeng's Huge Investment in Humanoid Robotics

## Extract

> In China there are many futuristic EV makers with bold ideas about innovation and the future of hardware. Alibaba backed Xpeng has the distinction of making prototypes of both flying cars and humanoid robots. In fact, we might want to pay attention, Xpeng’s chairman recently said it plans to start mass production of its flying car model and industrial robots by 2026.This is also part of the Alibaba stock story, because Alibaba owns a significant 7.5% stake in XPeng Inc.
