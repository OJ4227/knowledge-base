# Anysphere

## Summary

<!-- curated -->

Developer-tools company, maker of **Cursor**, an AI-native code editor. Founded in 2022 by
a group of MIT graduates; headquartered in San Francisco.

## What they do

<!-- curated -->

Builds Cursor, a code editor (a fork of VS Code) with deeply integrated AI assistance —
inline edits, codebase-aware chat, and increasingly autonomous multi-step coding agents.
One of the fastest-growing developer-tools companies by revenue. Depends on frontier models
via API rather than training its own. Its agent features are a large-scale proving ground
for [[Function calling]] and a live case study for the [[Agent reliability]] thread.
