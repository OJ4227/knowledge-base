# Figure AI

## Summary

<!-- curated -->

Humanoid robotics company developing general-purpose bipedal robots for labour
applications. Founded in 2022 by [[Brett Adcock]]; headquartered in Sunnyvale, California.

## What they do

<!-- curated -->

Designs and builds humanoid robots (the Figure series) aimed first at manufacturing and
logistics work, with the home as a longer-term target. Develops its own vision-language-
action models for robot control, having previously collaborated with an external
foundation-model partner. Operating at the frontier of [[Humanoid manipulation]], and has
run commercial pilots with BMW.
