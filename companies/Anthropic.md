# Anthropic

## Summary

<!-- curated -->

AI safety and research company, creator of the **Claude** family of large language models.
Founded in 2021 by siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei together with a group of former OpenAI
staff. Known for a safety-forward research agenda — Constitutional AI, mechanistic
interpretability, and public work on scaling policy.

## What they do

<!-- curated -->

Develops frontier language models offered through an API and through consumer and
enterprise products. Published the **[[Model Context Protocol]]** in 2024 as an open
standard for connecting models to external tools and data. Active in interpretability and
alignment research. A central player in the [[Agent reliability]] thread.
